# DaxAlgo Expert Model Coordinator
Status: blocked

## Goal
Research current model-distillation and LLM-coordinator practice, then design and implement a server-hosted DaxAlgo expert model path for accurate, efficient strategy authoring across C#, quantitative finance, probability, risk, portfolio management, UI, and the private DaxAlgo architecture.

## Hypothesis (files/symbols)
- Extend the existing Pro-only `TradingTerminal.Ai.Coordinator` rather than creating a second orchestrator.
- Keep DaxAlgo architecture material, training data, prompts, evaluation sets, model adapters, and serving policy private; do not place them in `public/`.
- Start with a licensed open-weight coding base, retrieval over versioned DaxAlgo knowledge, teacher-generated/verified SFT data, and evaluation gates before any fine-tuning or promotion into the existing credit-charged service.
- Likely surfaces are the coordinator contracts/library/CLI/tests, private platform API metering, and authored documentation under `docs/`.

## Questions and answers
- The existing coordinator is authoritative until current source/tests prove otherwise.
- A trained checkpoint cannot be claimed until a rights-clean corpus, compute target, base-model license, training run, and held-out evaluation evidence exist.
- Server-owned credit reservation and settlement are already implemented. The expert model must reuse them; the desktop remains untrusted for model, price, usage, and charge authority.
